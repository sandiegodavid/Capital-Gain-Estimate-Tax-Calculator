# State tax treatment follow-up

The current planning formula applies the federal capital-loss carryover rule to state estimates.

- California and New York generally conform to the federal treatment.
- Some states, such as New Jersey, do not allow capital-loss carryovers.
- Some states are more generous; Alabama may allow a full net-capital-loss deduction against ordinary income in one year.

Add state-specific carryover rules and authoritative state-tax-source validation before relying on these estimates for filing or payment decisions.
